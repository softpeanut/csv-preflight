Product and license

The Personal tier costs 15,000 sats and licenses one user. The Team tier costs 100,000 sats and licenses up to 10 named users in one legal organization. Both tiers provide the same completed CSV Preflight Pro offline batch ZIP; only the license scope differs. Each license is non-exclusive and non-transferable. Buyers may use output commercially when they have the right to use the source data. The software or modified copies may not be resold, redistributed, shared beyond the licensed users and organization, sublicensed, or made available to third parties.

Processing contract

One run accepts 1–20 UTF-8 CSV files, up to 10 MiB each and 50 MiB combined. It creates comma-separated UTF-8 normalized files, per-file issue reports, and a manifest. The archive includes a browser interface and a Node.js 20+ CLI. The CLI can emit JSON, returns exit 0 for clean results, 1 for validation issues or rejected input, and 2 for invocation/runtime errors, and refuses to overwrite an existing output path. Parsed data rows are not deleted, padded, or truncated. Unsupported encodings, empty files, and unclosed quoted fields receive reports but no normalized output. Shopify profiles are bounded checks, not an import guarantee.

Order and delivery

A public order request is not payment and must contain no data or private information. After confirming availability, the seller posts an issue-bound, single-use BOLT11 invoice for 15,000 or 100,000 sats according to the selected tier, with a 30-minute payment window. The returned invoice status endpoint lets the seller verify settlement without asking the buyer for public payment proof. Within one business day of verified payment, the issue author's GitHub account is invited to a buyer-only private repository containing the licensed ZIP and SHA-256 checksum.

Support and refunds

Report a reproducible download or execution defect in the buyer-only repository within seven days. If it cannot reasonably be resolved, the paid sats are returned to a compatible Lightning invoice supplied by the buyer. Mandatory consumer-protection rules override conflicting terms. Preferences about normalization or rejection by an external importer are not guaranteed product defects.

Privacy and safety

The product has no upload, network request, analytics, cookie, account, or storage path. Public order issues must not contain data, filenames, contact details, payment proof, credentials, or private links. Do not process personal, financial, health, authentication, or other sensitive or regulated data. GitHub, Stacker News, and the Lightning network apply their own terms and privacy practices.
